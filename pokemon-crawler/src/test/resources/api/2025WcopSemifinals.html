Logo by Pissog
Hosted by ken, Vertigo, Syrinix, UT, goldmason, and mimi. Also, Larry.

~Spreadsheet~​

We've made it to the Semifinals of this year's WCoP! Coming in hot and fresh off a quarterfinals tiebreak win is Italy, now with two undefeated, 5-0 players, Santu and Empo, as well as two 4-0 players, Kebab mlml and zS. Paired with and defeating US West in the semifinals last year, this year they're going up against Chile, who've made it to the semifinals for the first time since their inception with a pretty incredible run themselves, especially when you look at their individual records being spread more evenly across the team, though Endill's on a hot streak with their own 4-0. US West and US Northeast are facing off for the second year in a row in the playoffs, where last year US West conquered their eastern brethren and moved on to the semifinals, and US Northeast returns to the semifinals for the first time since 2021. Each team has had some of their own standout runs so far: 4-0 streaks from fade for USNE and Frito and Fusien for USW, as well as emforbes' 3-0 for USW.

What do you expect to see from the potential finalists? 3 of these teams won trophies in back to back to back years-US Northeast in 2018, US West in 2019, and Italy in 2020-and only Italy has returned to the finals since. What do you expect to see from team Chile in their semifinals debut? As always, time will tell, so FIGHT FOR YOUR COUNTRY!

SV OU: Attribute vs. lax - both of these players are SV aces but I think Attribute feels more consistent as of late: that said, WCOP is historically one of lax's stomping grounds and I'd not be surprised if he found some quality tech for an opponent he's familiar with.
SV OU: Fusien vs. Star - Fusien looks damn good this tour. I'm really enjoying the stuff he's bringing; he's making me pull up the calc pretty frequently. The game vs java was a little lucky but you can't hold that against him. starmaster is starmaster, you're not getting a bad performance from him, but the hot hand is real.
SV OU: emforbes vs. blunder - this fight has existential stakes. If someone loses this and it ends up mattering they'll never hear the end of it on future laxcasts. Both players are coming off of wins in quarters but I liked blunder's win the best, just brought a tryhard team and played it pretty tightly. This is like 49/51 at most, hoping for a nice long-ish game.
SV OU: TJ vs. bhkg - I like bhkg's team choices a bit more than TJ's so far, he's clearly a grinder (he got tourbanned for grinding too hard!) and has seemed generally in touch with the meta. TJ counters this with a 3-1 record of his own and much more experience in high-pressure situations. Purely in terms of odds this is close but I'm actually gonna predict this being the shortest game of the set.


Italy (3) vs. (2) Chile
SV OU: Niko vs. Lazuli - I've liked Lazuli's games thus far, one of the key pieces spearheading this Chile run. Definitely somebody I'll be watching out for after WCOP. Niko is a very strong player but IMO he needs this win to keep his momentum positive. I'm hoping for him to get to his HO bread-and-butter; let's get an Iron Moth at team preview.
SV OU: H.M.N.I.P vs. azogue - I'm not overly familiar with either of these players' games specifically in SV OU, I think HMNIP was in UU before? I'm feeling a Dudunsparce attempt here followed by no full paras on important turns.
SV OU: zS vs. Mister McLovin - zS has maybe the hottest hand in SV this tour. 4-0, strong games throughout (that game vs Unamed was a heater), selected for tiebreaks amidst a stacked roster. Not many I'd bold against him right now - Mister McLovin has had some nice brings so far but it's going to take a big effort.
SV OU: Tricking vs. Mako - highlight matchup. The Tricking SV arc has been all wins so far, and now he's facing one of Chile's aces in Mako. She pilots a variety of strong teams and is usually fairly consistent across disciplines. This one could go either way but Tricking's x-factor just narrowly gives him an edge imo, he's a real trickster and I'm not sure how you would prep for him in SV besides generic anti-Italy prep.
SV OU: Kebab mlml vs. Abele - Kebab is 4-0 bringing weird stuff all four times...he's sheist. This is nowhere near unwinnable - Chile as a whole has punched above their weight, and there's a non-zero chance that the Italian cheese goes stale - but Kebab is undeniably the favourite.
