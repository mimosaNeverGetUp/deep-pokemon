Incredible team. You earned a lot of respect from me when you beat me in the PU Open.

My suggestions are mostly set tweaks with the sets I use:
  • Glowking I normally run Psychic Noise over Psyshock just to prevent recovery. The EV spread in the paste is optimized to outspeed Toxapex and Garganacl so it can win that 1v1 with the remaining defensive EVs aligning with the Smogdex analysis. I run Surf on mine for Heatran especially because of Tera Water on Glowking, but have no complaints if you are attached to Ice Beam.
  • Great Tusk I put Earthquake over Body Press for better coverage and consistent STAB. Headlong Rush works for similar reasons. I also added some speed for Jolly Kingambit. Normally I would run Roar on this set, but Rapid Spin and Stealth Rock in one set is too valuable. Ghost Tera wins spin wars against other Great Tusk.
  • Moltres feels unsettled even though I wouldn't remove its role on this team. Will-o-Wisp certainly works, but I find over time that Flamethrower plus Flame Body burns anything that I need anyway. Roar is nice to prevent Dragonite, Zamazenta, and Ceruledge from setting up. U-turn is nice to get Jolteon back in the match, but feel free to keep Scorching Sands if Ceruledge and Heatran are problems. The spread here is really unorthodox, but it guarantees a OHKO on uninvested Rillaboom and a 2HKO on uninvested Great Tusk with Flamethrower. Speed EVs again for Jolly Gambit.
The team is solid enough to where none of these changes are necessary, but they would be to my taste. Piloting a Jolteon team to #1 is not easy and I applaud you for your efforts. Well done!
