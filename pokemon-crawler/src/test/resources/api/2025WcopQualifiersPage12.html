As someone who hasn’t participated in this qualifying but was heavily invested into team NL performance, here is my take.

To follow a precedent set back in 2012 is just mindboggling to me. There have been plenty of suggestions by players that actually got community support and weren’t just for their own gain.

I understand reluctancy to not change something in the middle of it happening. #24TeamWCOP, albeit being a bit of a meme, shows that the scene has competatively grown enough where we can have a format where 24 teams realistically are good enough to not water the format down.


The format might also be one of the worst things I’ve ever seen and can potentially be biased against teams who did well in the Ubers/RoA metagames. A team being able to send in just one player is kind of crazy in my eyes too, it’s a team format first and foremost.

I’m not going to say what solution is the best but this decision just further strengthens the #24TeamWCOP shouts. Come up with a good one, or have an open discussion with all managers (including the ones from the main tour to avoid all the quali managers just trying to get themselves to autoqualify). This situation shouldn’t be solved with a 2012-ruling where a lot of people on this forum didn’t even have their first pubes yet.


Also don’t even get me started about the fact there are act wins. This needs to be adressed for next edition.


Tl;dr: Out of all possible solutions I think you picked the worst one. I’d totally understand if there are teams boycotting this one. Then it’d make the farce complete.
